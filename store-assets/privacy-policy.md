# Currency Lens — Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 8 July 2026*

Currency Lens does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal data.

## What the extension does

When you highlight text on a webpage, Currency Lens checks — locally, on
your device — whether the selected text contains a price. If it does, it
shows the converted amount in a tooltip. The selected text never leaves your
device.

## Data stored

- **Your settings** (home currency and symbol preferences) are stored in
  your browser's extension storage. If you are signed into your browser,
  the browser may sync them across your devices; this sync is handled
  entirely by your browser vendor, not by us.
- **Cached exchange rates** are stored locally for up to 12 hours.

## Network requests

The extension makes requests to exactly two services, solely to download
current exchange rates:

- `https://open.er-api.com` (ExchangeRate-API open endpoint)
- `https://api.frankfurter.dev` (Frankfurter, ECB reference rates)

These requests contain no personal information, no browsing history, and no
page content — they simply fetch the daily rate table.

## What we never do

- No analytics or telemetry
- No tracking of browsing activity
- No reading of page content beyond the text you explicitly select
- No accounts, no ads, no sale of data
